# 0015 OpticFilm 120 support in pyopticfilm: identity, open, feed, home

Status: in progress

Owner's direction (2026-10-15): results in the driver, not more experiments. The two proven replay tools (`tools/pi/vendor_open.py`, `tools/pi/move_carrier.py --as-move-1` and `--home`, `0011-first-motor-move.md`) become pyopticfilm code on a feature branch of the fork (`DESIGN.md`), so that from here on every run on the unit is a pyopticfilm run. Scanning is the next item and needs the "Scan" section of `0012-driver-playbook.md` first.

Design, decided 2026-10-15 (`doc/log/2026-10-15.md`):
- Identity: `07b3:1406` at any bcdDevice, and the Genesys default `05e3:0103` only together with bcdDevice `0x0702`, the owner's unit (`doc/hardware/usb.md`). `list_devices` enumerates both vendor ids. Closes the open decision in `DESIGN.md`.
- Model: `device/model_opticfilm_120.py`, a standalone frozen dataclass, not a `Gl128Common` leaf: the sibling catalog in pyopticfilm is for the three 35 mm models that share captures, and the OpticFilm 120 shares neither geometry nor open nor motor with them. `asic = "GL126"` (the chip on the board, `doc/hardware/board.md`). `scan_ready = False`, so every scan path stays closed; `Scanner.arm_bringup_motor()` is the explicit arming step before a feed or a home, which matches the go-ahead rule. `init_regs` is the capture's blast (118 pairs); the clock pair, DRAM refresh, lamp-off, AFE defaults, eject select and standby pulse are model fields with their sources.
- ASIC class: `asic/gl126.py`, `Gl126(Gl128)`, dispatched by `asic == "GL126"` as `Gl843V1` is for the 7600i v1. Overrides `asic_boot` (the engine's open as `doc/protocol/playbook.md`, "Open", part 4 lays it out), `init` (no table upload), `feed` (move 1's registers, table and cut), `home` (the same with `MTRREV` and the host's stop at the home sensor), `park`, `stop_motor`, plus `release_standby` (the engine's `Unregister`, not wired into close yet).
- Oracle: register-level equality with the proven tools. Tests replay the fake transport and compare the ordered `(register, value)` pairs, the `0x8c` writes and the AHB uploads with the capture's frames, not transfer boundaries, which the ASIC does not see.
