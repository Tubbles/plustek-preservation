# 2026-10-14

## The flash does not hold the identity #flash #diagnosis

With the owner's go-ahead, `tools/pi/flash_dump.py` read the whole serial flash (EON EN25F40, 512 KiB) in 35 s, two identical passes. It holds the infrared record at address 0 and nothing else (`doc/hardware/flash-dump.md`). That rules out the counter reset as the cause and the flash as the place the USB identity lives on this unit. Next candidate: a serial EEPROM on the ASIC's EEPROM interface, which Plustek's engine can read (`CAsic::ReadFromNVRAM`) but never does. Board photos and a read over USB are the next steps. The USB read means register writes beyond the SPI requests, so it needs its own review and the owner's approval.
