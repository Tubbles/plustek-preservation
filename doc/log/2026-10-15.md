## Reassembled, mechanics unverified #hardware #mechanics #safety

The owner reassembled the unit after the board photos. The spring-loaded gear train around the moving holder carrier may not be back as it was, and one screw hole is stripped (`doc/hardware/board.md`, "Reassembly"). Decision: no motor-driving request goes to the unit until a mechanical check has been agreed and done (`doc/work/0010-mechanical-check.md`), and the film holder stays out, since the firmware ejects an inserted holder on its own once a host connects. The EEPROM read and keep-awake write no motor register, so they go ahead as planned, each with the owner's go-ahead.

## EEPROM read over USB does not reach the chip #eeprom #diagnosis

With the owner's go-ahead, `tools/pi/eeprom_read.py` ran once on a fresh power-up (2026-10-14 22:08 UTC, `doc/hardware/eeprom-read.md`): every word `0000`, and the data registers held `00` before the first command. That is the result the exposure-register reading predicted, so the USB route to U19 is closed by the rule in `doc/protocol/serial-eeprom.md`. Next: a read in circuit with an SOIC-8 clip on the Pi's GPIO, which is a hardware purchase and the owner's decision.

## Keep-awake enabled #standby

`keep-awake-enabled` now exists on the Pi, so the watcher runs `tools/pi/keep_awake.py` on every wake. Whether the unit then stays on the bus past five minutes is checked on the next wake. The flag has to go before any further `eeprom_read.py` run.
